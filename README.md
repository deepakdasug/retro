# Retrotech - Business Made Easy

A modern, high-converting digital landing page built with pure **HTML5**, **Vanilla CSS**, and **Vanilla JavaScript**.

---

## 📁 Where to Upload Your Files

### 1. 🖼️ Where to Upload Your Logo
- **Upload Directory:** `assets/images/`
- **File Name:** `logo.png` *(or `logo.svg`)*
- **Path:** `retro_digital/assets/images/logo.png`

> The site includes a crisp SVG and PNG fallback for Retrotech so it looks sharp immediately until you place your file.

---

### 2. 🎬 Where to Upload Your Background Video Clip
- **Upload Directory:** `assets/videos/`
- **File Name:** `background-clip.mp4`
- **Path:** `retro_digital/assets/videos/background-clip.mp4`
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264)

> When you place your video clip here, it will automatically play across the full screen with the royal blue gradient overlay.
